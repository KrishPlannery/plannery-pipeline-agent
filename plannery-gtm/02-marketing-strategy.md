# Marketing Strategy
## Plannery GTM Reference — Channels, Cadence, and Agent Execution

---

## Strategic Objective

Marketing supports the HealthStream channel. It does not supplant it.

Three specific jobs:
1. **Pre-sell the room** — before a HealthStream AE walks in, HR already has vague positive awareness of Plannery so the AE is not starting from zero
2. **Unstick stalled deals** — systematic nurture keeps deals warm without requiring AE bandwidth
3. **Arm the AE** — assets sharp enough that the conversation converts

**Timeline reality:** The goal is 26 hospitals closed and launched by end of 2026, approximately 35 weeks from now. Pipeline can grow rapidly; closes cannot — cycles are 6–18 months. The math only works if most of the 26 are already in pipeline at some stage. The first action before any outreach is a pipeline audit: count deals at each stage in Attio, identify which are stalled, calculate how many net-new accounts need to enter pipeline to hit 26 closes at a realistic conversion rate per stage.

## Channel 1: HealthStream AE Enablement

**This is the most important channel by an order of magnitude.**

HealthStream AEs have quota on HealthStream products. Plannery is a favor they do, not a number they make. Without deliberate enablement, they deprioritize within 60 days of launch regardless of what the partnership agreement says.

The goal: make Plannery the easiest upsell the AE has ever attached to a conversation.

### Assets to Build

**The 60-second verbal hook** — not a script, a hook:
> *"One thing a lot of our hospital partners have been asking about lately is nurse retention. Are you feeling that pressure? We actually have something embedded directly in HealthStream that several systems have turned on and seen real results — zero IT lift, no cost to the hospital. Worth 15 minutes?"*

**The leave-behind one-pager** — after the hook lands, the AE emails it same-day. See `ae-one-pager.md`. Designed so HR can forward it to their VP without editing it.

**The objection card** — five objections with exact response language. AEs will use a one-page cheat sheet. A 40-page playbook they will not.

**Quarterly AE briefing** — 20 minutes, new customer wins, updated retention data, new objection patterns. Keeps Plannery top of mind.

### Critical Metric

Negotiate this into the HealthStream partnership at the Nashville meeting: **number of Plannery introductions per AE per month.** Without this metric, there is no visibility into whether the channel is working until pipeline is already dry. If AE introduction rates cannot be tracked, the channel cannot be managed.

### The Nashville Meeting

A deck is being prepared for the meeting with HealthStream's CEO, COO, and key leaders. The goal of that meeting is not relationship-building — it is walking out with: agreed AE introduction rate metrics, an agreed tracking mechanism, named AE champions for a pilot, and a joint QBR cadence. Goodwill without operational structure produces zero AE behavior change.

## Channel 2: Direct Outreach to HR Leaders

**Goal:** When a HealthStream AE mentions Plannery, HR says "I've heard of that" — not "what's that?" That shift in recognition meaningfully increases AE conversion rates.

This channel does not close deals. It pre-sells the room.

### Target List

HR Directors, VP Benefits, and Chief People Officers at HealthStream accounts not yet in pipeline. Pull from Attio and supplement with LinkedIn. Agent 6 handles list building and outreach execution.

### LinkedIn Organic Content

Krish posts 2x per week as founder — not as Plannery brand. Founder-led content outperforms brand content in B2B by a wide margin in trust-deficit categories like fintech.

**Content mix:**
- 40% — Data and insight posts (lead with a surprising Nursegrid or Plannery outcomes finding, explain it, close with implication for HR leaders)
- 30% — Customer story posts (specific numbered outcomes, not testimonial quotes — narrative)
- 20% — Reframes and provocations (challenge conventional assumptions about financial wellness or nurse retention)
- 10% — Founder perspective (humanization and trust)

**Format rules:** No more than 5 sentences per paragraph. No bullet-point lists in post body. End with a question or observation that invites response. No explicit CTA asking people to book a demo.

**Production system:** Agent 4 drafts two posts per week. Krish edits. 20 minutes per week. No production system means exclude this channel entirely — inconsistency compounds negatively with LinkedIn's algorithm.

### Direct Email Sequences

Three-email sequence to HR leaders at HealthStream accounts not yet in pipeline. Agent 6 personalizes each sequence using Agent 1's hospital research output.

| Email | Timing | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Email 1 | Day 1 | Nursegrid financial stress insight relevant to their hospital type. No product mention until the final line. |
| Email 2 | Day 9 | Phoebe Putney case study outcome with specific numbers. One sentence on what Plannery did, one sentence on the result. Offer to share the full case study. |
| Email 3 | Day 17 | Short direct ask. "If nurse retention isn't a priority right now, just say so and I'll stop. If it is, I have 20 minutes of data worth your time." |

Stops after Email 3 unless engagement. Contacts who open but do not reply after Email 2 or 3 are flagged to Krish — warm but unconverted contacts get a separate handling decision.

## Channel 3: Pipeline Rescue

**This is the most urgent revenue problem.** Deals going quiet is not a minor operational inconvenience — it is how winnable deals are permanently lost in a 6–18 month sales cycle.

### Pipeline Stage Definitions

| Stage | Definition |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Initial conversation had; follow-up scheduled or pending |
| Stage 2 | Demo done or outcomes brief sent; awaiting response |
| Stage 3 | Internal champion identified; awaiting C-suite approval |
| Stage 4 | Cold/holding — escalated based on rules below |

### Escalation Rules

- Stage 2 with no email response or open tracked by Attio for **14+ days** → move to Stage 4
- Stage 3 with no movement for **28+ days** → move to Stage 4

Stage 4 is the cold/holding stage. All rescue sequences run from Stage 4.

### Stage 4 Rescue Sequence

| Touch | Timing | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Touch 1 | Week 1 of Stage 4 | Nursegrid financial stress insight relevant to their hospital type and size |
| Touch 2 | Week 3 of Stage 4 | Dollar value calculation using NSI 2026 numbers sized to their specific employee count (pulled from Attio) |
| Touch 3 | Week 6 of Stage 4 | "Permission to close your file" email — creates loss aversion, reliably generates responses from stalled deals |
| Touch 4 | Week 10 of Stage 4 | Move to quarterly re-engagement track |

**Every touch delivers value. No "just checking in" emails. Those get deleted.**

### Human in the Loop

Every drafted rescue email is presented to Krish for approval before sending. Present: deal name, hospital name, current stage, days since last contact, Attio email engagement status (opened/not opened/replied), the draft email, and a one-line rationale. Do not auto-send under any circumstance.

## Channel 4: Content Marketing

### Primary Data Asset: Nursegrid Financial Survey

The Nursegrid financial survey (approximately 9,000 nurse respondents, November 2024) is the primary content foundation. Agent 5 analyzes this dataset and surfaces insights about financial stress as a hidden driver of nursing turnover. These insights feed LinkedIn posts, email sequences, and the Nursing Financial Stress Report.

### The Phoebe Putney Case Study

Webinar recording and images from Phoebe Putney Memorial Hospital are in `/plannery-gtm/`. Build a structured two-page case study: hospital profile, the retention problem they had, what they did, specific outcomes at 90 and 180 days. This becomes the primary AE leave-behind, Email 2 attachment in the outreach sequence, and LinkedIn content.

### Annual Nursing Financial Stress Report

Use Nursegrid survey analysis plus Plannery's anonymized outcomes data. Target distribution: Becker's Hospital Review, HealthLeaders, Nurse.com. This is the single highest-leverage content asset because no one else has this data. First edition anchored on Nursegrid survey findings; subsequent editions incorporate Plannery hospital outcomes data.

### Turnover Cost Calculator

Web tool: HR leader enters number of nurses and current attrition rate, gets a dollar figure for annual turnover cost and Plannery's estimated impact using NSI 2026's $295,000 per 1% figure. Gated behind email capture. Highest-converting lead gen asset because it delivers immediate personal value and creates a specific reason to follow up.

## What We Are Not Doing

| Channel | Why Not |
|---|---|
| Paid advertising (LinkedIn, Google) | Hospital HR does not make benefits decisions from ads. Wrong channel for this buyer. |
| Conferences (SHRM, ASHHRA) | Valid long-term but expensive and slow. Not a priority until AE channel is proven. |
| Webinars | Low ROI for time-starved hospital HR leaders. A 2-page brief they can read in 3 minutes outperforms a 45-minute webinar. |
| PR firm | Becker's and HealthLeaders accept direct contributor pitches from operators with genuine data. Not a PR firm job at this stage. |
